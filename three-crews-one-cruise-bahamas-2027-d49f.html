June 12 – June 20, 2027  |  Carnival Jubilee  |  Roundtrip from Galveston, Texas

Eight days, one ship, three cabins, ten people who all need to be somewhere at the same time — Galveston, June 12th, 3:30 in the afternoon. From there the Jubilee runs two sea days before the first port call, then hits Nassau, the private stop at RelaxAway on Half Moon Cay, and Celebration Key, before turning back toward Texas for two more days at sea and a final approach into Galveston on the 20th. Every family books its own cabin, every family pays its own way, everyone eats at the same buffet at some point.

Day-by-Day

  • June 12 — Galveston, Texas
    Departs 3:30 PM. Boarding, muster drill, and the first look at the ship before it pulls away from the Texas coastline.
  • June 13 — Fun Day at Sea
    No port, no schedule beyond the ship's own — pools, deck chairs, whatever's happening on board that day.
  • June 14 — Fun Day at Sea
    Second sea day before landfall in the Bahamas.
  • June 15 — Nassau
    8:00 AM – 5:00 PM. New Providence Island — straw market, colonial-era streets, and a working harbor with cruise ships lined up along the pier.
  • June 16 — RelaxAway, Half Moon Cay
    8:00 AM – 5:00 PM. Carnival's private out-island stop — a crescent of beach with none of the port-town noise.
  • June 17 — Celebration Key
    8:00 AM – 4:00 PM. Carnival's newer private destination in Grand Bahama, built specifically for this kind of stop.
  • June 18 — Fun Day at Sea
    Turnaround toward Texas begins.
  • June 19 — Fun Day at Sea
    Final full day on board before disembarkation prep.
  • June 20 — Galveston
    Arrival and disembarkation.

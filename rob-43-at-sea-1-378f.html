The only official plan

Mr. Sanchos Beach Club, Cozumel. An all-inclusive beach club on the island’s west shore: lounge chairs in the sand, food and drinks included with the day pass, and the birthday crew in one place. The day pass is booked separately from your cruise; Tabitha can help arrange it.

Everything else is your vacation. Join in as much, or as little, as you’d like.

The route

Seven nights, three ports, back home to Galveston.

  1. Galveston, TexasSun, Mar 28 · Depart

    Boarding day. Drop your bags, find the pool deck, and watch the island shrink as you sail out past the jetties.

  2. Roatán, Honduras

    Green hills dropping into clear water over the Mesoamerican Barrier Reef. Snorkeling here is some of the best on the route; West Bay Beach is a short ride from the pier.

  3. Puerto Costa Maya, Mexico

    A purpose-built port with pools and shops at the pier. Walk or cab to the fishing town of Mahahual for beach bars, or ride inland to the Maya ruins at Chacchoben.

  4. Cozumel, MexicoFri, Apr 2 · Birthday beach day

    Mr. Sanchos day. Shallow turquoise water off the beach, and San Miguel’s waterfront for tacos and souvenirs if you peel off afterward.

  5. Galveston, TexasSun, Apr 4 · Return

    Disembarkation morning. Plan on being off the ship and on the road by mid-morning.

Days at sea fall between ports. Exact port arrival and departure times come with your booking confirmation.

Life aboard Symphony of the Seas

One of the largest ships afloat, laid out in neighborhoods so it never feels like one crowded deck.

Central Park

An open-air garden with real trees and plantings, lined with cafés and restaurants. The quiet middle of the ship.

The Boardwalk

A hand-carved carousel, candy shop, and the AquaTheater, where high divers perform at night.

Ultimate Abyss

A dry slide that drops ten decks from the top of the ship to the Boardwalk. Fast and loud.

FlowRider & zip line

Two surf simulators on the top deck and a zip line strung across the Boardwalk, nine decks up.

Pools & slides

Multiple pools, an adults-only solarium, and twisting waterslides for the afternoons at sea.

Bars & shows

The Rising Tide Bar moves between decks, robots mix drinks at the Bionic Bar, and Broadway-style shows run most nights.

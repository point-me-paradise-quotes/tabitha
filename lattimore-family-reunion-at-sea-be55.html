The ship

MSC Seascape is a Seaside-class ship built for warm-water itineraries, with a promenade that brings you down close to the waterline rather than keeping everyone up on a pool deck. For a multigenerational group, that matters: the grandparents can claim shaded loungers by the rail while the kids disappear to the waterslides, and everyone regroups at dinner. Sailing from Galveston also means no flights for anyone who can drive to the port.

A few things worth knowing

What the promotion tags on each sailing actually mean.

Kids Sail Free

On select sailings, eligible children traveling as the third or fourth guest in a stateroom may sail free or at a reduced cruise fare. Taxes, fees and restrictions still apply.

Free Balcony Upgrade

Select sailings offer Balcony staterooms at Ocean View prices. Where it applies, the promotional rate is already reflected in the price shown.

Onboard Credit

Spending money loaded to your onboard account. The amount depends on the promotion and stateroom category.

Drinks & Wi-Fi

Some promotional fares bundle drinks and Wi-Fi. We’ll compare those against adding the packages separately, rather than assuming either way.

What about group rates?

A group rate isn’t automatically the better deal.

Once you pick a preferred date, I’ll contact MSC to review group pricing and any extra amenities that come with it. Then I’ll put it side by side with booking each family unit on its own reservation.

Traditional group booking

Everyone under one group contract. Can bring group pricing and added amenities, and keeps the family’s rooms managed together.

Individual reservations

Each family books separately. This can unlock offers like Kids Sail Free, onboard credit and Drinks & Wi-Fi packages that may be worth more than the group rate.

Either way, the family sails together on the same ship and the same week. We’ll compare both before deciding how to move forward.

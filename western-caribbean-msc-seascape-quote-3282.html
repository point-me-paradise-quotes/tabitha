Your sailing: MSC Seascape

Three sea days, three ports, and a ship big enough that you’ll still be finding corners on day six.

You board in Galveston on a Sunday afternoon, sail out at 4:00 p.m., and spend two slow days crossing the Gulf before the ship docks at Costa Maya, Roatán and Cozumel on three mornings in a row. One more day at sea, and you’re back in Galveston at 6:30 a.m. the following Sunday. Seascape is a Seaside Class ship from MSC Cruises, built around the water: a boardwalk that wraps the lower decks outdoors, an infinity pool off the stern, and two glass walkways you can stand on and look straight down at the sea.

All three ports are docked calls, so you walk straight off the ship — no tender boats. Costa Maya is your long day ashore, with ten hours alongside. Port times can change; your advisor will check them again at booking.

Life on board

The ship’s signature pieces are outdoors: Robotron, a robotic-arm ride that MSC says is the only one at sea; an infinity pool at the stern; the Bridge of Sighs and the Infinity Bridge, two glass walkways; and the waterfront boardwalk. Beyond those you’ll find an aquapark, a ropes course, a racing simulator, virtual-reality games, an XD interactive cinema, a sports court, a silent disco and dueling pianos.

In the theater, the shows on this ship are Dreamscape, Imaginocean, In Concert, Love Blooms, Premiere and Young at Heart. There are 9 places to eat — including Aurea Restaurant, reserved for Aurea guests like you — and 18 bars and lounges, among them Le Cabaret Rouge (a two-level 1920s-style lounge), Sky Bar, Jungle Beach Bar, the Sports Bar, and Venchi chocolate and gelato bars.

Drinks Not included

Bought one at a time, beer runs about $6–8 and cocktails about $8–13, with a 15% service charge reported on each order. On U.S. sailings MSC’s only alcohol package is Premium Extra, about $70–95 per person per day: up to 15 alcoholic drinks a day plus soda, juice, coffee and bottled water, service charge included. It must be bought for every day of the cruise and doesn’t cover room service or the minibar. The Alcohol-Free package is offered below as a pre-paid add-on.

Specialty restaurants Not included

Butcher’s Cut steakhouse, Hola! Tacos & Cantina, Ocean Cay seafood, Kaito Sushi Bar and Kaito Teppanyaki. Single meals run about $25–55; bundles are about $99 for 2 meals, $139 for 3 and $179 for 4, tips included, drinks extra. Butcher’s Cut can be used once per bundle. (2025 prices)

Wi-Fi Not included

Two plans, Browse and Browse & Stream, each tied to one device. Buying before you sail saves up to 20% — it’s offered below as a pre-paid add-on.

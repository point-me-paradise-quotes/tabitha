The area

Two stretches of coast are in play here. Playa Mujeres sits north of Cancún proper, a quieter run of beach where Excellence and Atelier both sit — about a 15–20 minute drive from the ferry and the mall in Puerto Juárez. The Zona Hotelera, where Sandos is, is the busier strip closer to La Isla Shopping Mall and Delfines Beach, with more foot traffic and a shorter run to the clubs if that's part of the plan. All three properties below are adults-only and all-inclusive, and each one is priced for the two of you for the same three nights, January 11–14, 2027.

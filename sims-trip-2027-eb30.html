Option 1 of 3

Excellence Playa Mujeres

Punta Sam, CancunAdults-only450 suites~15 min from the ferry dock

This is the most all-suite, most polished of the three - every room is a suite, the balconies and patios are furnished rather than an afterthought, and there are nine restaurants on property so you're not eating the same buffet twice. It sits on its own stretch of Playa Mujeres, about a 15-minute drive from the Ultramar ferry dock and the Cancun Mall, and roughly 18 miles from the airport. If your group wants a slower pace and doesn't mind staying put most of the trip, this is built for that.

Dining & drinks

  • 9 restaurants (8 a la carte, 1 buffet) plus 24-hour room service
  • 11 bars and lounges, unlimited premium drinks
  • Minibar restocked daily, in-room coffee/tea maker

Pools, beach & activities

  • 7 outdoor pools and a private beach setting
  • Water sports: kayaking, windsurfing, snorkeling gear, intro scuba lesson
  • Full-service spa, 24-hour fitness center, yoga, sauna/Turkish bath
  • Cooking, dancing and Spanish lessons; nightly theme shows

Not included

  • Airport transfer (shuttle available for a surcharge)
  • Spa treatments beyond what's listed above

Option 2 of 3

Atelier Playa Mujeres

Playa Mujeres, Quintana RooAdults-only (18+)9.6/10, 1,470 reviews17-min walk to Mujeres Beach

This one leans into the food. Thirteen restaurants on site, including Maria Delores, which holds a Michelin star, plus an art gallery and a Nuup Spa with 18 treatment rooms. It reads more date-night than girls'-trip on the surface, but the pools, poolside bars, and beach setup are every bit as good, and it's the highest-rated of the three properties here. Worth knowing going in: it's a quieter, more romance-oriented atmosphere than Sandos.

Dining & drinks

  • 13 restaurants, including Maria Delores (Michelin-starred)
  • 12 bars/lounges including 2 swim-up bars and 2 coffee shops
  • Unlimited drinks poolside
  • Vegetarian and vegan options throughout

Pools, beach & activities

  • 4 outdoor pools, 1 hot tub, 1 heated pool
  • Free cabanas, loungers and umbrellas, pool and beach
  • Health club with Pilates and fitness classes, free bike rentals
  • Evening entertainment and an on-site art gallery

Not included

  • Airport transfer
  • Nuup Spa treatments (massages, facials, hydrotherapy - reservations required, 18+)
  • Golf at the adjacent Playa Mujeres Golf Club
  • Pet fee: $50/pet/night if applicable

Option 3 of 3

Sandos Cancun All Inclusive

Zona Hotelera, CancunAdults-only (18+)Property class 4.5Near La Isla Mall & Delfines Beach

This is the one your advisor's specialist pointed toward, mainly because it's the only property of the three with a room built to fit all of you together - a two-bedroom Caribe Suite that sleeps six. Neither Excellence nor Atelier had a room configuration that put four people in one space for these dates. Sandos sits right in the Zona Hotelera near La Isla Mall, has a nightclub and theme nights if the group wants a livelier evening, and comes in as the mid-priced pick of the three. Tabitha hasn't stayed here personally, but it was recommended directly by the specialist who works this destination.

Dining & drinks

  • 5 restaurants: international buffet, beachfront Caribbean, Asian teppanyaki, fine dining, and Italian
  • 3 bars/lounges plus a snack bar/deli
  • 24-hour room service, in-room minibar included

Pools, beach & activities

  • 3 outdoor pools plus a hot tub, all renovated in 2017
  • Free cabanas, loungers and umbrellas on the beach
  • Non-motorized water sports, fitness classes, yoga, tennis
  • Nightclub and live evening entertainment

Not included

  • Airport shuttle and area shuttle (both surcharge)
  • Motorized water sports
  • Spa treatments (13 treatment rooms on site, reservations required)
  • Late check-out (fee applies, subject to availability)

Resort Option 1

Secrets Baby Beach Aruba

Adults-only · San Nicolas, Aruba

This one opened in 2025 on the quieter southeastern tip of the island, a five-minute walk from the shallow, current-free water at Baby Beach. It's adults-only, all-suite, and built around three separate pool scenes rather than one central one — an infinity pool facing the water, a livelier social pool, and a Preferred Club pool that stays noticeably calmer. Thirteen restaurants and bars are spread across the property and none of them take reservations, so dinner is whatever you're in the mood for that night, not what you booked three weeks ago.

  • Seven restaurants and six bars — South American, Italian, Pan-Asian, seafood — walk-in only
  • Three pools plus two outdoor hot tubs; Preferred Club suites add private plunge pools
  • Secrets Spa and an air-conditioned fitness center on site
  • Short walk to Renaissance Island and a few minutes' drive from San Nicolas
  • Top-shelf spirits, tips, and taxes all folded into the rate

Resort Option 2

Tamarijn Aruba All Inclusive

Family-friendly, oceanfront · Oranjestad, Aruba

Tamarijn sits directly on the same 1.5-mile stretch of white sand that TripAdvisor readers voted the Caribbean's best, right next door to its more upscale sister property, Divi Aruba — and you get full run of both. It's a livelier, more casual property than Secrets: a 30-foot rock-climbing wall on the beach, five freshwater pools (plus two more shared at Divi Dutch Village), a kids' club for ages 4–12, and a weekly artisan fair. Note the front desk lists a minimum check-in age of 21 for the person booking the room, but this is not an adults-only resort — families are very much part of the scene here.

  • Oceanfront rooms with a private patio or balcony, steps from the beach
  • 12 restaurants and grills, 8 bars, shared across Divi & Tamarijn
  • 5 freshwater pools plus 2 more at Divi Dutch Village
  • Sea Turtles Club for kids 4–12; nightly entertainment and a weekly artisan fair
  • E-mountain bikes, pickleball, tennis, and a shuttle running between both properties

The ship

Carnival Jubilee is a 2023 Excel-class ship that sails from Galveston and runs on liquefied natural gas.

Jubilee leaves from Galveston, so for a lot of this group the trip starts with a drive down I-45 rather than a flight. Once you’re aboard, the ship is laid out in six themed zones — a central hub for shows and coffee, a dining strip with Emeril Lagasse’s bistro, a boardwalk-style stretch called The Shores, the pool deck, and a top-deck playground with a roller coaster and water slides. It’s a big ship, so expect lines at the buffet on sea days and plenty of quiet corners if you go looking.

What costs extra on board

None of what follows is in the fare; it goes on your onboard account or is bought before you sail. Tap any item for details.

Crew gratuities Not included$102 per person to prepay, or charged on board

You can prepay them with your booking at $102 per person for the sailing, or leave them to be added to your onboard account automatically. They apply to every guest age 2 and up.

20% service charge Not includedOn drinks, specialty dining and other paid food

Added to drinks, specialty dining and other paid food. It went up from 18% in December 2025.

Shore excursions in Cozumel and Roatán Not included · optionalBooking and refund terms

Online booking closes 24 hours before sailing, and cancelling before then gets a full refund. Cancelling on board costs 25%, and excursions can’t be refunded within 24 hours of arriving in port. If Carnival or the tour operator cancels, you’re refunded.

Other paid extras Not included · optionalBOLT, Cloud 9 Spa, bar drinks
  • BOLT roller coaster: $15 per ride; riders 4′4″ to 6′5″, 300 lb limit (from a third-party source).
  • Cloud 9 Spa: guests 18+. Cancel 7 or more days before sailing for a full refund; cancelling on board with less than 24 hours’ notice costs 50%.
  • Drinks at Dr. Inks, The Golden Mermaid and the other bars, unless you have a package.

Good to know

The practical pieces worth settling before you commit.

Getting to the ship Not included

Travel to and from the Galveston cruise terminal, and parking there, are not part of this quote — you’ll arrange those on your own. No flights or transfers are included.

Embarkation & return

The ship sails at 3:30 PM on Sunday, April 25, and arrives back in Galveston at 8:00 AM on Saturday, May 1. Allow time on return for re-entry formalities before you head home.

Travel documents

Requirements depend on each traveler’s citizenship. Check Carnival’s travel-document guidance and travel.state.gov, and confirm with your advisor well before sailing.

Age rules

Serenity and CHEERS! are 21+. Cloud 9 Spa is 18+. Gratuities apply to every guest age 2 and up.

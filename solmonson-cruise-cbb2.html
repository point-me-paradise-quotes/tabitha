The Ship

What you'll find aboard Carnival Breeze, deck by deck.

Carnival Breeze sails roundtrip from Galveston over five days and two ports. Onboard, expect SportSquare's ropes course and mini-golf, a 320-foot Twister Waterslide at the ship's water park, and dining that ranges from the family-style Italian courses at Cucina del Capitano to Guy Fieri's barbecue at the Pig & Anchor Smokehouse. Staterooms and public spaces carry a warm, tropical-toned decor throughout.

Public spaces are spread out: the water park, pools, and casual dining sit on the Lido deck (10); specialty dining, bars, the casino, and shops line the Promenade deck (5); the spa, sports deck, and the adults-only Serenity retreat sit up top (decks 12–15); and staterooms fill most of decks 1–2 and 6–11 across interior, ocean view, and balcony categories.

Dining Aboard

A quick tour of where to eat, from included to specialty.

Seaday Brunch

A late-morning spread that runs breakfast into lunch — eggs several ways, French toast, loaded mac and cheese. Included.

Main Dining Room

Full table service, three courses a night, included in your fare. The menu mixes Carnival standards with dishes developed alongside Emeril Lagasse, plus a dedicated vegan menu. Pick Early Dining, Late Dining, or Your Time Dining when you book.

Mongolian Wok & The Captain's Pasta Bar

Build-your-own noodle or pasta bowls — choose your base, sauce, and protein. Part of the included Lido dining.

Lido Marketplace

Casual buffet-style dining open for breakfast, lunch, and dinner: a deli counter, salad bar, an Asian station, pizza, and soft-serve. Included.

Cucina del Capitano

An Italian trattoria serving family-style courses inspired by the ship's Italian officers. Reservations recommended, not required.

Guy's Pig & Anchor Bar-B-Que Smokehouse

Open-air barbecue with Guy Fieri's recipes — pork butt, smoked beef, andouille sausage. Included.

Fahrenheit 555 Steakhouse & Bonsai Sushi

Specialty options aboard. Ask your advisor about à la carte pricing.

The Chef's Table

A multi-course dinner with a galley tour, hosted by the ship's executive chef for a group of 12–16. Reservations open after booking; seating is limited.

Coffee Bar

Specialty coffee, tea, and pastries.

Entertainment & Onboard Life

Beyond the dining room, here's how the days and nights fill up.

The Punchliner Comedy Club

Stand-up nightly, with separate family-friendly and adults-only sets. Check the Carnival Hub App or the daily Fun Times for showtimes.

The Atrium

The ship's central, multi-level gathering space — live music or a DJ, events through the day, and the Guest Services desk.

Video Arcade

Open 24 hours, all ages.

The Fun Shops

Duty-free and tax-free shopping — jewelry, cosmetics, watches, liquor, apparel, and accessories.

WaterWorks

The ship's water park, built around the Twister Waterslide.

Playlist Productions

Live musical stage shows; the lineup varies by sailing.

Alchemy Bar & BlueIguana Tequila Bar

Themed cocktail bars, including a non-alcoholic cocktail option at Alchemy Bar.

Pixels

The ship's photography team, with photo packages available.

Kids & Teens

Age-grouped programming for younger travelers in your party.

Camp Ocean (ages 2–11)

Supervised, age-grouped activities — Penguins (2–5), Stingrays (6–8), Sharks (9–11) — with arts and crafts, games, and kid-friendly mealtimes. Included.

Night Owls (11 and under)

Supervised late-night activities until 1 a.m. A fee applies (amount not yet specified).

Circle "C" (ages 12–14)

A dedicated lounge and activities — dance parties, games, outdoor movies.

Club O2 (ages 15–17)

Movies, music, sports, video games, and karaoke with other teens.

ZSPA

Spa services geared toward teens — hairstyling, pedicures, and fitness classes.

Health & Fitness

For the adults looking to slow down or work out.

Serenity Adult-Only Retreat

A quieter, adults-only deck area on the same ship.

Cloud 9 Spa

Massages, facials, body wraps, and thermal suites. Additional cost.

Fitness Center

A full gym plus scheduled fitness classes.

Hair and Beauty Salon

Standard salon services, plus teeth whitening.

SportSquare

Mini-golf, a ropes course, a jogging track, a convertible basketball/volleyball court, and gym equipment on the open deck.

Thursday, February 4

Galveston, Texas

Departs 3:30 p.m.

Galveston sits about 50 miles from Houston, with a different pace and feel of its own. Moody Gardens is the main family draw, and the restored Galveston Island Historic Pier is worth a walk if you have time before boarding.

Friday, February 5

At Sea

An open day — nothing booked. Time for the pool, SportSquare, or a conference session.

Saturday, February 6

Cozumel, Mexico

Arrives 9:00 a.m. · Departs 5:00 p.m.

Cozumel sits off Mexico's Yucatán Peninsula, known for clear water and reef diving along the Mesoamerican Reef. Beyond the water there's the San Gervasio ruins, the market streets of San Miguel, and the Punta Sur eco-park for wildlife and beach time. Shore excursions and time ashore are not included in your cruise fare.

At Sea

Another open day at sea — nothing booked.

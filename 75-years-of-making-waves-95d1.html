Royal Caribbean, Carnival or MSC

Royal Caribbean

A mix of activities, entertainment, dining and downtime for families and multigenerational groups.

  • Large, activity-filled ships
  • Broadway-style and production entertainment
  • Multiple complimentary and specialty dining options
  • Activities for children, teens and adults
  • Each generation can do its own thing and still vacation together
  • Symphony of the Seas: larger, resort-style ship
  • Liberty of the Seas: more traditional experience on a smaller ship
Per person · double occupancy

Carnival

Casual and lively, an easy fit for families and groups celebrating together.

  • Fun, relaxed atmosphere
  • Family-friendly activities and entertainment
  • Comedy, live music and production shows
  • Multiple casual dining options
  • Water attractions and pool areas
  • The widest range of Galveston sailing lengths and price points
Per person · double occupancy

MSC Cruises

An international-style experience on modern ships, with entertainment, dining and family activities.

  • Modern ship design
  • International onboard atmosphere
  • Multiple dining and entertainment options
  • Family and kids programming
  • Pools, water attractions and activities
  • MSC Seascape sails 7-night itineraries from Galveston
Per cabin · double occupancy

Reading the prices: All rates are based on double occupancy. MSC rates in this proposal are per stateroom (cabin). Royal Caribbean and Carnival rates are per person. Don’t compare MSC’s cabin rate side by side with the per-person rates; they measure different things.

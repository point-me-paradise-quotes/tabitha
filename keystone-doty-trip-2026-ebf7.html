Hotel options

Two ways to do Keystone at Christmas

Both stays keep you in Keystone, but they shape the trip differently. Hidden River Lodge makes the ski-school part easy and keeps the setting quieter. River Run Village puts you in the center of the lights, restaurants, and village movement. The right choice depends less on star rating and more on how you want the day to flow.

Option 1 - Hidden River Lodge

Hidden River Lodge

Keystone, CO · December 19, 2026 - December 24, 2026
Flight + selected room
$4,436

How this stay feels

This is the calmer side of Keystone: easier mornings, less foot traffic, and a shorter walk when ski school is the center of the trip. It trades nightlife and restaurant density for convenience on beginner terrain, which can matter more than atmosphere when five people are managing gear, lessons, and December temperatures.

What is included

Room basis: Room only - no meals included in the rate

  • Located in Keystone's Mountain House Base
  • Perfect for beginners because the ski school is a short walk from the lodge
  • Quiet area close to the Discovery learning area
  • 0.2 miles from Magic Carpet
Room category

Suite 2 bedroom, 2 bath

$2,347 total

This layout is built for flexibility rather than symmetry. One bedroom is comfortably adult-oriented with the king bed, while the second leans family-style with the pyramid bunk. The sofa bed adds overflow sleeping in the common area, useful for a group of five that does not need identical sleeping arrangements.

Sleeping setup

  • Bedroom 1 - 1 King Bed
  • Bedroom 2 - pyramid bunk (double bottom, twin top)
  • Living area - double sofa bed
  • Two bathrooms

Why pick this option

  • Best fit for a group prioritizing ski school access and a quieter base area
  • Less immediate restaurant and shopping energy than River Run
  • Simple room-only setup, so breakfasts, lunches, and dinners are separate costs

How this stay feels

River Run puts you in the middle of the village energy: lights, storefronts, easy coffee stops, and a more social end to the ski day. The trade is that convenience here is about village life and gondola access, not the ultra-straightforward beginner setup of Mountain House.

What is included

Room basis: Room only - no meals included in the rate

  • Great location for shopping and restaurants
  • Lively Christmas atmosphere
  • Gondola access
  • Still beginner friendly, though it may require a little more navigating
  • Free shuttle
Room category

$3,093 total

This option is about being in River Run as much as the room itself. The Buffalo Lodge two-bedroom condo gives the group apartment-style breathing room, but the stronger selling point is stepping out into the village instead of retreating to a quieter base after skiing.

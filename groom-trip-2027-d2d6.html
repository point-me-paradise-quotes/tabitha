Three islands to choose between

We put together three adults-only Sandals resorts across three islands, each with a different feel. Grande St. Lucian is the busiest of the three — more restaurants, more pools, a golf course next door. Grande Antigua trades some of that density for the longest beach on the island. Sandals Grenada is the quietest, tucked into a secluded cove on Pink Gin Beach. Below, each resort has its own room categories priced out for you — pick the resort first, then the room that fits.

All of the room rates below are priced for two adults, for the full four nights, April 19–23, 2027. Across the six rooms we priced, totals range from $7,476.36 to $12,549.80 for the stay.

Resort Choice 1

Sandals Grande St. Lucian

Gros Islet (Rodney Bay), Saint Lucia — adults-only, couples-only

Grande St. Lucian sits on its own peninsula in Rodney Bay, with the Atlantic on one side and the Caribbean on the other, and the Pitons visible in the distance on a clear day. It's the busiest of the three: 12 restaurants, 7 bars, five pools, and a full water sports program that includes PADI-certified scuba and green fees at the 18-hole Cap Estate Golf & Country Club next door. The mile-long beach runs the length of the property, and the resort's overwater bungalows — glass panels set into the floor, private soaking tubs, butler service — sit just off it. The "Stay at 1, Play at 3" program also gets you into two more nearby Sandals resorts for the day, so a change of scenery is a shuttle ride away rather than a flight.

What's included

  • Breakfast, lunch, dinner, and snacks at 12 specialty restaurants
  • Bottomless top-shelf spirits and Robert Mondavi wines at 7 bars, including a swim-up bar; stocked in-room minibar
  • PADI-certified scuba diving, snorkeling and equipment, water skiing, wakeboarding, Hobie Cats, paddleboards, kayaks
  • Green fees at the 18-hole Cap Estate Golf & Country Club, plus round-trip shuttles there
  • 5 pools, 3 whirlpools, 1 scuba pool, tennis and other land sports, fitness center
  • Day and night entertainment
  • "Stay at 1, Play at 3" access to two nearby Sandals resorts
  • All taxes and gratuities, round-trip airport transfers, Wi-Fi

Not included

  • Red Lane Spa treatments (bookable add-on)
  • Local excursions and tours off-property (e.g. Soufrière/Pitons trips, ziplining, dune buggy adventures)
  • Helicopter airport transfers (available at extra cost)

Resort Choice 2

Sandals Grande Antigua

St. John's, Antigua — adults-only, couples-only

Grande Antigua fronts Dickenson Bay, the longest beach on the island, with beachfront suites and cottages at one end and hillside oceanview suites at the other. It runs slightly leaner than Grande St. Lucian — 11 restaurants instead of 12, six pools instead of five — but it makes up for it with more open sand and its own take on the private-pool suite: thatched-roof Rondoval cottages and one-bedroom Butler Villas with a plunge pool built into the patio. Certified divers get scuba included, with a longer stretch of open water out front for it.

What's included

  • Breakfast, lunch, dinner, and snacks at 11 specialty restaurants
  • Bottomless top-shelf spirits and Robert Mondavi wines at 7 bars, including a swim-up bar; in-room minibar
  • PADI-certified scuba diving (certified divers), snorkeling and equipment, Hobie Cats, paddleboards, kayaks
  • 6 pools, 6 whirlpools, tennis and other land sports, fitness center
  • Day and night entertainment
  • All taxes and gratuities, round-trip airport transfers, Wi-Fi

Not included

  • Red Lane Spa treatments (bookable add-on)
  • Excursions off-property (e.g. Xtreme Circumnavigation Tour, rainforest zipline, sunset catamaran cruise)
  • Private candlelight dinner (bookable special experience)

Resort Choice 3

Sandals Grenada

St. George's, Grenada — adults-only, couples-only

Sandals Grenada sits on Pink Gin Beach, a secluded stretch with cliffs and coves on either side and views out toward distant mountains and rainforest. It's the smallest and quietest of the three — 10 restaurants, 6 bars, five pools — and built around privacy: Skypool Suites with their own infinity-edge plunge pools over the water, and a shared pool area with a fire pit the resort calls its "living room in the pool." If the first two resorts sound like more activity than you want, this is the one that trades some of it for space and quiet.

What's included

  • Breakfast, lunch, dinner, and snacks at 10 specialty restaurants
  • Bottomless top-shelf spirits and Robert Mondavi wines at 6 bars, including a swim-up bar; in-room minibar
  • PADI-certified scuba diving (certified divers), snorkeling and equipment, Hobie Cats, paddleboards, kayaks
  • 5 pools, 2 whirlpools, tennis, pickleball and other land sports, fitness center
  • Day and night entertainment
  • Arrival and departure airport lounge
  • All taxes and gratuities, round-trip airport transfers, Wi-Fi

Not included

  • Red Lane Spa treatments (bookable add-on)
  • Excursions off-property (e.g. historical sightseeing tour, rainforest hike, river tubing, snorkeling cruise)

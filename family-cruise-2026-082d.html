Four December sailings, one port to start from

Every sailing leaves from and returns to Galveston, and every one stops in Cozumel. Two are on Royal Caribbean's Liberty of the Seas and two are on Carnival Breeze. They differ in length (Sailing C is four nights, the other three are five), in the other ports they add, in what happens around Christmas, and in what the fare covers.

Read the fare rows as a starting point, not a like-for-like ranking — nights, ships and inclusions differ. On the numbers above, Sailing A has the lowest Interior fare per person of the four ($484), and Sailing C is next lowest ($636).

Which Cruise Line Fits Your Family?

Since this will be your family's first cruise, both Royal Caribbean and Carnival are great places to start. While they offer many of the same cruise basics, the overall experience and atmosphere can feel a little different.

Royal CaribbeanCarnival Cruise Line
The VibeFamily-friendly, active and a little more polishedFun, casual and energetic
The ShipsThe ship itself is a big part of the vacation, with lots to exploreEasygoing ships built around fun, food and entertainment
ActivitiesRock climbing, surf simulators, ice skating and more, depending on the shipWaterslides, mini golf, pools, games and activities, depending on the ship
EntertainmentBroadway-style productions, ice shows and other ship-specific entertainmentProduction shows, comedy, trivia, games and interactive entertainment

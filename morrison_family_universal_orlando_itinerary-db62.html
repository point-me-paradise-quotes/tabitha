If something comes up, here's who to call first

  1. Any emergency or safety concern: call 911 right away. Park and resort team members can also get help to you quickly.
  2. Room, check-in, parking, or anything at the hotel: start with the front desk of the hotel you're staying at that night.
  3. Tickets, Express Passes, or something in the parks: visit Guest Services inside the park or call Universal Guest Services.
  4. Still stuck, or something about your reservation isn't right: call or text Tabitha and we'll work it out with you.

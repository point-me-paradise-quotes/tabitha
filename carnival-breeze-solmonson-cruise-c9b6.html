Breeze is a ship built around choices: SportSquare and a 320-foot Twister Waterslide up top, RedFrog Rum Bar and BlueIguana Cantina by the pool, Guy's Burger Joint and Guy's Pig & Anchor Bar-B-Que Smokehouse for casual meals, and Cucina del Capitano and Fahrenheit 555 Steakhouse when you want a sit-down dinner. What's on boardDining, entertainment, kids & teens, fitness, deck guide

Dining on Carnival Breeze

1 / 4
  • Dining room dinner. Three courses, table set for you, included in your fare. Choose Early Dining or Late Dining for reserved seating, or Your Time Dining, when you book. Included
  • Seaday Brunch. Huevos rancheros, special French toast, loaded mac-n-cheese and a lot of egg preparations.
  • Lido Marketplace. Barbecue, a deli counter, salad bar, Asian station, pasta bar and pizza, with indoor and outdoor seating.
  • Guy's Pig & Anchor Bar-B-Que. Open-air smokehouse: pork butt, smoked round of beef, andouille sausage, Blue Ribbon Chicken, mac n cheese, collard greens.
  • Cucina del Capitano. Italian dishes served family-style, inspired by the captains' and officers' childhoods. Reservations are not required but recommended; make one online after booking.
  • Mongolian Wok and The Captain's Pasta Bar. Pick your noodles or pasta, your sauce and your protein and the chef cooks it to order.
  • The Chef's Table. A multicourse dinner hosted by the Master Executive Chef for 12–16 guests, with a champagne toast and a galley tour. Seating is limited; reserve online after booking.
  • Coffee Bar. Specialty coffee, tea, pastries, milkshakes, cake and cookies.

Any extra charge for specialty venues, the Chef's Table and the coffee bar is not stated in our research; your advisor will confirm.

Entertainment

  • Punchliner Comedy Club. Some shows are family-friendly and some adults-only; check the Carnival Hub App or the daily Fun Times.
  • Playlist Productions and Lip Sync Battle. Song-list production shows with singers and dancers; titles vary by ship.
  • Liquid Nightclub, Alchemy Bar and BlueIguana Tequila Bar. Alchemy Bar is a vintage-pharmacy-themed cocktail bar with seven non-alcoholic drinks made with Lyre's spirits; BlueIguana sits next to the pool.
  • WaterWorks. Twister Waterslide, DrainPipe, PowerDrencher and Speedway Splash; configurations vary by ship.
  • The Atrium and the Video Arcade. Live music or a DJ in the Atrium, where Guest Services is also found; the arcade is open 24 hours.
  • The Fun Shops and Pixels. Duty-free and tax-free shopping, and onboard photographers with photo packages. Purchases are extra.

Kids & teens

  • Camp Ocean. Supervised, age-based groups: Penguins 2–5, Stingrays 6–8, Sharks 9–11.
  • Night Owls. Late-night supervised parties for ages 11 and under, until as late as 1 a.m. Fees apply
  • Circle “C”. For ages 12–14: dance parties, games, outdoor movies.
  • Club O2. For ages 15–17: movies, sports, video games, karaoke.
  • ZSPA. Spa and salon treatments made for teens.

Health & fitness

  • Serenity Adult-Only Retreat. A quiet deck area away from the rest of the ship, with a bar nearby.
  • Cloud 9 Spa and Hair & Beauty Salon. Massages, facials, body wraps, thermal suites; hair, nails and teeth whitening at the salon.
  • Fitness Centre and SportSquare. Fitness classes, bikes, ellipticals and weights, plus mini-golf, a ropes course, a jogging track and a basketball court that converts for soccer and volleyball. SportSquare offerings vary by ship.

Day by day

Five days: two on land, two at sea, one in between.

Feb 4 · Thursday — Galveston, TexasDeparts 3:30pm

Departs 3:30pm. Galveston is about 50 miles from Houston, and it feels like a different place. Moody Gardens is the biggest draw, and the refurbished Galveston Island Historic Pier is a good place to spend time before you sail.

Feb 5 · Friday — At seaA day at sea

A day at sea. Nothing is booked for this day in this quote.

Feb 6 · Saturday — Cozumel, MexicoArrives 9:00am · Departs 5:00pm

Departs 5:00pm | Arrives 9:00am. Cozumel sits off Mexico's Yucatán Peninsula, known for clear water and the Mesoamerican Reef, which draws snorkelers and divers. On land there are the Mayan ruins at San Gervasio, the markets in San Miguel, beaches, and the Punta Sur eco-park.

Shore excursions are not included in this quote. Not included Cozumel is an international port of call, so allow extra time for border formalities and check official government sources for the travel documents each traveler needs; your advisor can point you to them.

A day at sea. Nothing is booked for this day in this quote.

Feb 8 · Monday — Galveston, TexasArrives 8:00am

Arrives 8:00am. The ship is back in Galveston. Travel home from the port is not part of this quote.

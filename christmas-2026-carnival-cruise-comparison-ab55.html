Which Ship Offers the Best Experience?

Best for Amenities & Accommodations

Carnival Jubilee

The newest and largest of the three ships, featuring more dining options, entertainment, attractions, and the exclusive Havana experience. It is also the only option currently showing an aft-facing balcony.

Best for a Traditional Carnival Experience

Carnival Liberty

A smaller ship with classic Carnival entertainment, dining, and relaxation areas. It offers the same Bahamas destinations as Jubilee but departs from New Orleans.

Best for Western Caribbean Destinations

Carnival Dream

A great alternative for guests who prefer visiting Cozumel, Grand Cayman, and Jamaica instead of the Bahamas. It also offers the convenience of departing from Galveston.

My Recommendation

Carnival Jubilee is currently the strongest option, especially considering the available aft-facing balcony and Havana Cabana accommodations — both of which I currently have on hold for you.

For only $30 more per stateroom, the Havana Cabana offers an exclusive retreat experience with additional outdoor relaxation amenities.

If having a traditional aft-facing balcony overlooking the ocean is the priority, the Jubilee aft-facing balcony is also an excellent option.

I'll confirm availability with Carnival on the Liberty and Dream aft-facing balconies before ruling those options out completely.

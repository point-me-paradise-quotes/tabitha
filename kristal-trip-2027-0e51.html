Where You're Headed

Aruba sits south of the hurricane belt, which is part of why late May stays reliably clear — trade winds keep the heat honest, and the water off the west coast runs the pale turquoise you've seen in photos, minus the exaggeration. This is the low-key side of the Caribbean: no lush jungle, just cactus-studded desert meeting calm, current-free swimming.

Every resort on this list sits along the same stretch of coast between Palm Beach and Eagle Beach, so wherever you land, you're a short walk or a five-minute cab from the others if you want to bar-hop or check out a different pool scene one afternoon.

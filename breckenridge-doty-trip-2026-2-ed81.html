Hotel options

Both stays are in Breckenridge, but they solve the trip in different ways: one leans toward resort amenities and value, the other toward a prime Peak 9 address.

Option 1 | Beaver Run Resort

Beaver Run Resort

Breckenridge | December 19-24, 2026

Room only

The feel: Beaver Run is the more full-service, family-leaning choice. There is movement here - kids drifting toward the arcade, people in robes heading to the spa, skiers peeling off layers near the hot tubs after the lifts close. If you want the stay to carry some of the entertainment load on its own, this one does that better.

Location: This is the ski-in/ski-out pick, set up for easy slope access without giving up the ability to shuttle around town. It is a practical choice for a December ski trip with 5 travelers because the logistics stay simple once everyone is bundled up and moving at different speeds.

What is included - and what is not: The rate is room only. Meals are not included in the nightly price, which matters in Breckenridge over Christmas week when breakfast, lunch on the mountain, and dinners in town add up quickly.

Option 2 | The Village at Breckenridge

The Village at Breckenridge

Breckenridge - Base of Peak 9 | December 19-24, 2026

Room only

The feel: The Village is the address-driven option. The draw here is less about a long amenity list and more about the rhythm of the trip: out the door, onto Peak 9, then back toward Main Street without much friction. For travelers who care most about being right in the middle of Breckenridge's pedestrian energy, this one has the stronger position.

Location: Base of Peak 9 and close to Main Street means the mountain and town are both within easy reach. That combination tends to matter on a Christmas trip, when some of the best parts of the day are not only skiing but also walking to dinner, browsing shops, and not coordinating constant rides.

What is included - and what is not: Like Beaver Run, this rate is room only. No meals are bundled into the price, so the premium here is really being paid for location and access, not dining inclusions.

Recommended read on these choices

Best value: Beaver Run Resort. For this group size, the numbers are dramatically easier, the suite setup is functional, and the amenity list gives the trip more range after skiing.

Best address: The Village at Breckenridge. If the trip is less about stretching the budget and more about stepping out directly into Peak 9 and Main Street energy, this is the cleaner location play.

Big-picture tradeoff: both are room-only stays, so neither option softens your food budget. The difference is whether you want to spend on where you are in town, or on the overall trip experience elsewhere.

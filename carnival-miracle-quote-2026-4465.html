What's Built Into Your Fare

The fare covers a lot more than the room.

Included:

  • Your stateroom, with daily housekeeping, linens, climate control, TV, and an in-room safe
  • Meals whenever you're hungry across the Main Dining Room, the Lido Marketplace buffet, Seaday Brunch, Guy's Burger Joint, BlueIguana Cantina, Pizza Pirate, and Swirls
  • Non-alcoholic basics — lemonade, iced tea, hot chocolate, still water, and regular coffee and tea
  • Entertainment — Playlist Productions musicals, the Punchliner Comedy Club, Family Feud Live, and Seuss at Sea
  • Kids' and teens' clubs — Camp Ocean (ages 2–11), Circle "C" (12–14), Club O2 (15–17), and Space Cruisers
  • Pools, the WaterWorks water park, the Serenity Adult-Only Retreat, the fitness center, and Carnival's Players Club

One small note on dining: a second entrée in the Main Dining Room is on the house — a third comes with a $5 charge.

Taxes and port fees are already folded into the fare quoted below — you won't see them added again at checkout. Not included — gratuities are not part of the fare; see below.

What Costs Extra

Split into what's expected and what's optional.

Required — Gratuities Not included in fare

Carnival's crew gratuities aren't part of the cruise fare. The recommended rate is $17.00 per person, per day for Ocean View and Interior staterooms, or $19.00 per person, per day for suites — over this 4-night sailing that comes to $68 per person (Ocean View/Interior) or $76 per person (Suites). It's charged automatically to your Sail & Sign account unless you prepay at booking, and it can be adjusted at Guest Services once you're aboard. Everyone sailing is assessed except children under 2.

Optional — Onboard Extras

  • Drink package (Cheers!): $69.95 per person, per day if purchased before sailing ($74.95/day onboard), plus a 20% service charge — covers most drinks for the whole cruise, and has to be bought for the full sailing, not single days. A non-alcoholic version, Cheers! Zero Proof, starts at $29.99/day.
  • Specialty dining: the Steakhouse runs $52 per adult ($15 for kids 11 and under); the Chef's Table is $95–$124 per adult (12 and up); Bonsai Sushi Express is à la carte and its menu pricing isn't published — ask onboard.
  • Wifi: roughly $20–$28 per day depending on the plan, higher if you wait to buy it once you're sailing.
  • Shore excursions in Cozumel: priced per person and vary widely by tour, roughly $45 to $630+ — full refund if you cancel 24 hours or more before sailing.
  • Bars and spa treatments: pay as you go; bar tabs carry a 20% service charge.

Included with this booking, at no extra charge

A $50 onboard credit comes with this booking as a perk — it's not added to your total below, it's simply there to use once you're aboard.

What a starting rate covers

These numbers are for comparing sailings, not a final price. Here’s how to read them.

Generally included in the cruise fare

  • Your stateroom for the nights shown
  • Main dining room and buffet meals
  • Pools, onboard shows and kids’ programs
  • Stops at every port listed on the itinerary

Not included in these starting rates

  • Daily gratuities
  • Drink packages, specialty restaurants and Wi‑Fi
  • Shore excursions
  • Cruise port parking or travel to Galveston
  • Travel insurance

Taxes, fees and port expenses, plus pricing for a third or fourth guest in a cabin, will be confirmed line by line in your detailed quote once you’ve picked your favorites.
